    Our Story

    Welcome to Current, a leading name in the world of noodles and snacks, crafting culinary delights that resonate with flavors of innovatio n and quality. Established in 2017, our journey began in the heart of Nepal, where we set out on a mission to redefine the snacking experience.

    Current is a proud creation of Yashoda Foods Pvt. Ltd., a parent company with a legacy of culinary excellence. Established in Nepal, our brand has been on a journey to redefine the snacking experience since its inception in 2017. Two of our state-of-the-art manufacturing facilities in Rupandehi and Kapilvastu, Nepal are at the heart of our operations.
